So what makes Drishti different? First, physics and A I together: a mass-conserving model of the drains and the surface, with a fast neural surrogate on top. Second, it is probabilistic. Every forecast carries its spread, and the chance of crossing ten, twenty, or thirty centimetres. Third, it closes the loop. Live sensor readings correct the twin, and expose blocked drains. And fourth, it explains its reasoning, and turns forecasts into decisions.
